About Special Group Interested In Programming Contest

Special Group Interested In Programming Contest is KUET's competitive programming community focused on algorithmic excellence, ICPC preparation, and a healthy Codeforces culture. We train through topic-based sessions, contest simulations, post-contest upsolving, and peer mentorship to help every member level up with clarity and consistency.
