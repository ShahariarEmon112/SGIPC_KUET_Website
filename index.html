About SGIPC

Special Group Interested In Programming Contest

SGIPC is a dedicated community at KUET focused on competitive programming excellence. We organize contests, workshops, and mentoring sessions to help students develop their algorithmic and problem-solving skills.

  • Regular online and offline contests
  • Experienced mentors and guidance
  • Resource sharing and learning materials
  • Team-based training for ICPC
  • Portfolio building through competitions

Suggested Practice Roadmap

📌 Phase 1: Fundamentals (Weeks 1-4)

Master basic data structures and algorithms including arrays, strings, linked lists, and sorting techniques.

📌 Phase 2: Intermediate (Weeks 5-12)

Learn trees, graphs, dynamic programming, and greedy algorithms with real-world applications.

📌 Phase 3: Advanced (Weeks 13-20)

Study advanced topics like segment trees, advanced graph algorithms, and optimization techniques.

📌 Phase 4: Contest Preparation (Weeks 21+)

Participate in real contests, practice under time pressure, and refine your problem-solving strategies.
